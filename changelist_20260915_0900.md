## 0. Executive summary (CEO view)

The brief is "a software agency that uses AI to build and ship sites and software faster". The current site does not say that. It reads like a generic IT services template ("Built to ship. Built to last.", "Engineering partner, not a vendor"). AI speed appears only in one paragraph near the bottom of the About page.

The biggest problems, in order:

1. **Positioning gap.** Nothing above the fold says "AI-accelerated", "weeks not months" or gives a concrete speed or price promise. That is the one thing that sets this agency apart, and it is missing.
2. **Trust gap.** Testimonials are anonymous role labels ("Product Founder"), every case study page opens with "This case study is an illustrative placeholder", the About page shows empty leadership circles, social icons do not link anywhere, and the email is on another company's domain (`shridharinfosec.com`). A buyer will notice all of this.
3. **Conversion leaks.** The contact form opens the visitor's mail app (`mailto:`), so on most phones and work laptops the lead is lost. The only CTA is "Get a Quote". There is no booking link, WhatsApp or phone CTA on mobile.
4. **Broken mobile menu.** Once the page is scrolled, the mobile menu collapses to a 76px strip (measured). Most phone visitors cannot navigate after scrolling.
5. **Content that goes blank.** Headings and whole sections stay invisible until two stacked scroll animations finish. In captures, FAQ questions, case study headings and a Why Us card title were blank, and stats showed "0".

Fix P0 first, then the positioning copy (Section 2), then the section-by-section UI work.

## 1. P0: Critical bugs (fix first)

### P0-1. Mobile menu collapses to header height after scrolling

- **Where:** `components/layout/Navbar.tsx`, `components/layout/MobileDrawer.tsx`
- **What happens:** `MobileDrawer` is rendered inside `<header>`. When `scrolled` is true, the header gets `backdrop-blur-md`. `backdrop-filter` creates a containing block for `position: fixed` children, so the drawer's `fixed inset-0` becomes relative to the header. Measured at 390px and 768px with the page scrolled 1500px: dialog height 76px, viewport 640px. The menu is unusable.
- **Fix:**
  1. Render `MobileDrawer` outside the `<header>`: either move it next to `<header>` in `Navbar.tsx` (return a fragment), or portal it to `document.body` with `createPortal`.
  2. Also lock body scroll while the drawer is open (`document.documentElement.style.overflow = "hidden"`, restored on close).
- **Check:** at 390px, scroll 1500px, open the menu. The dialog height must equal the viewport height and the service accordion must scroll inside it.

### P0-2. Stacked scroll animations hide content

- **Where:** `components/shared/ScrollReveal.tsx` (adds `.sr` to every `h2` and `h3` in `#main-content`), `components/shared/SectionReveal.tsx` (wraps every home section), `app/page.tsx`, `app/globals.css` (`.sr`, `[data-reveal]`)
- **What happens:** Each home section fades in with `SectionReveal`, and each heading inside it also fades in with `ScrollReveal` plus a stagger delay of up to 360ms. On fast scrolls, anchor jumps, "back" navigation, screenshots and Lighthouse runs, headings stay at opacity 0. Accordion questions are `h3`, so FAQ rows show as empty lines. It also causes a very "templated" feel.
- **Fix:**
  1. Delete `ScrollReveal` from `app/layout.tsx` and remove the component and the `.sr`/`.sr-in` CSS.
  2. Keep one reveal: `SectionReveal`, using `IntersectionObserver` with `rootMargin: "0px 0px -10% 0px"`, 12px translate, 400ms, fired once.
  3. Never animate text inside accordions, cards or carousels.
  4. Keep the `prefers-reduced-motion` override.
- **Check:** Jump straight to `/#faq` and take an immediate screenshot. All questions must be visible.

### P0-3. Stats render "0" before JavaScript runs

- **Where:** `components/home/StatsStrip.tsx`, `hooks/useCountUp.ts`
- **What happens:** The server HTML and the first paint show `0+`, `0`, `1+`. Crawlers, link previews and slow phones see "0 projects".
- **Fix:** Render the final value in the server HTML. Only animate from 0 on the client when the element enters view and reduced motion is off. Add `aria-label="60 plus projects in production"` so screen readers get the final number once.
- **Check:** `curl` the home page and confirm `60+` appears in the HTML.

### P0-4. Contact form loses leads (`mailto:`)

- **Where:** `components/contact/ContactForm.tsx`, `lib/mailto.ts`
- **What happens:** Submitting opens the visitor's email app. On phones without a mail app set up and on most office laptops, nothing happens and the enquiry is lost. The UI even says "if it doesn't, email us directly instead".
- **Fix:**
  1. Replace `submitContactForm` with a real submission. The site builds as static export on Netlify and natively on Vercel, so pick one:
     - Option A (works on both): a form service such as Formspree, Web3Forms or Basin via `fetch` POST.
     - Option B (Vercel only): a Route Handler at `app/api/contact/route.ts` that sends email with Resend. Read the route handler docs in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` first, and drop `output: "export"` for Vercel only.
  2. Add a hidden honeypot field and a simple time-to-submit check for spam.
  3. Add states: idle, sending (button disabled with a spinner), success (inline panel: "Thanks, [name]. We will reply within one business day. Want to talk sooner? Book a 20 minute call."), and error (with a fallback email link).
  4. Add fields that help qualify leads: "What are you building?" (select: Website, Web app / SaaS, Mobile app, AI feature or agent, Something else), "Budget range" (select), "When do you want to launch?" (select). Keep name, email and message required. Keep phone and company optional.
  5. Add `autoComplete` attributes (`name`, `email`, `tel`, `organization`).
- **Check:** Submit at 390px. A success panel appears and the email arrives. Nothing opens a mail app.

### P0-5. Email address is on a different company's domain

- **Where:** `data/siteConfig.ts` (`email: "info@shridharinfosec.com"`), shown in the TopBar, footer, mobile drawer, contact page and JSON-LD
- **Problem:** A software agency site using a cybersecurity firm's email domain looks like a mistake or a front.
- **Fix:** Switch to `hello@shridhartechnologies.com` (or similar) once the domain mailbox exists. `[OWNER TO CONFIRM]`

### P0-6. Hero carousel swaps the H1 every 8 seconds

- **Where:** `components/home/Hero.tsx`, `components/home/HeroTitle.tsx`
- **Problems:**
  - The page's only `h1` changes every 8s. Search engines and screen readers get an unstable main heading, and each slide replays its entrance animation.
  - Slide 3's primary CTA ("See our work") and the secondary CTA ("View Portfolio") both link to `/portfolio`.
  - The carousel has no pause control, which fails WCAG 2.2.2 for auto-moving content that runs longer than 5 seconds.
  - All three hero photos (250KB to 540KB each) load as CSS backgrounds, so the LCP image is not preloaded.
  - At 768px a capture landed mid-transition and showed a faded, empty hero.
  - The progress dots are 8x8px tap targets.
- **Fix:** Replace the carousel with one static hero (copy in Section 2.1). Use a single optimized image (AVIF/WebP, under 150KB) rendered as `<img fetchPriority="high">` or `next/image` with `priority`, or drop the photo and use a product-style visual (see 2.1). Remove the slider controls and the per-word animation on the H1. A single fade of the whole block is enough.

## 5. Accessibility

1. **Carousel pause** (P0-6). Auto-rotating hero with no pause fails WCAG 2.2.2. Removing the carousel fixes it.
2. **Tap targets.** Measured at 375px: 57 interactive elements on the homepage are smaller than 40px in one dimension, including hero dots (8x8px), marquee links (20px tall), footer links and inline arrows. Set a minimum of 44x44px hit area on phones (padding, not larger text) for all buttons, dots, icon links and footer links.
3. **Small uppercase button text.** All `Button` variants use 12px uppercase with `tracking-[0.08em]` and `font-extrabold`. That is hard to read. Use 14px, sentence case, `font-bold`, `tracking-normal` in `components/shared/Button.tsx`. This affects every CTA site-wide.
4. **`aria-label` on non-interactive spans.** Social placeholders use `<span aria-label="LinkedIn (coming soon)">`. Screen readers ignore `aria-label` on a plain span. Hiding placeholder icons (2.6 item 6) removes the issue.
5. **Heading outline.** Hidden mobile drawer `h3` elements come before the `h1` (3.2). Footer column titles are `h3` with no `h2` in the footer. Use `h2` with the same styling, or `p` elements.
6. **`aria-current` on dots.** `aria-current={i === index}` renders `aria-current="false"` on inactive items. Use `aria-current={i === index ? "true" : undefined}`. Goes away with the carousel and slider removal, but check other places.
7. **Mega menu trigger.** A link with `aria-expanded` that navigates on click is confusing for keyboard and screen reader users (see 3.2).
8. **Focus styles.** Nav links (`nav-underline`) have no `focus-visible` ring. Add `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-cyber rounded-sm`.
9. **Color contrast to check:** `text-mist` on `bg-deep` in the footer bottom bar; faint ghost numbers and `text-line-bright` labels; hero eyebrow text over photos. Target 4.5:1 for body text.
10. **Reduced motion.** Keep all existing `prefers-reduced-motion` handling when simplifying animations, and also disable the marquee and pulse animations there (already partly done).

## 6. Performance

The live page made the browser renderer unresponsive twice during screenshot capture on desktop. Likely causes are several full-screen effects at once. Fix these:

1. **Images:** `public/images/*.jpg` are 196KB to 540KB each (`meeting.jpg` 540KB, `servers.jpg` 437KB) with `images: { unoptimized: true }`. Convert to AVIF/WebP at 1600px max width (target under 150KB), add `width`/`height`, and use `srcset` sizes for phones. Hero images are CSS backgrounds, so the LCP image is discovered late. Use an `<img>` with `fetchPriority="high"` for the hero.
2. **Hidden images:** The About collage loads 3 desktop images and 3 mobile images (6 requests for 3 photos) because both layouts are in the DOM. Render one layout with a `<picture>` or CSS grid that changes with breakpoint.
3. **Continuous effects:** Infinite marquee animation, pulsing map pins, full-screen SVG patterns in hero, mega menu and testimonials, `backdrop-blur` on the sticky header, and `will-change` on every heading. Remove the pins and heading reveals, stop the marquee (3.4), and keep one pattern at most.
4. **Fonts:** Three Google font families (Montserrat 3 weights, Nunito Sans, JetBrains Mono 3 weights). Drop JetBrains Mono (used only in footer headings and the design-system page) and limit Montserrat to 700 and 800.
5. **Home page length:** 15,467px tall on a 375px phone, 11,390px at 768px. After the changes above (merge stats into hero, compact tech wall, 3-card portfolio grid, shorter process, no Why Us banners), target about 9,000px on phones.
6. Run Lighthouse (mobile) before and after. Targets: LCP under 2.5s, CLS under 0.1, TBT under 200ms.
